Generate & select

Describe the prop, figure, or set piece your scene needs — or upload your own reference. FLUX.2 Pro returns a set of variations. Pick the takes that work; convert several at once.

An optional background-removal pass gives the 3D conversion a clean plate to work from. Skip it when your renders already sit on a plain background.

Straight into your scene

Trellis-2 builds production-ready GLB models from your selects. GLB imports natively into Blender, Unreal, Unity, Maya and more — download and block your scene while the idea is still fresh.
